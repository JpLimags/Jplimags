## 👋 About

I'm a data scientist working with **fraud detection and credit studies in the health insurance sector**, and I'm finishing my Computer Science degree at **IFCE (Maracanaú)**.

I like applied machine learning: taking a messy real-world problem, building a reliable pipeline around it, and shipping something people can actually use.

- 🔭 Exploring: RAG systems and LLM-based applications
- 🎓 Thesis: predicting microbial growth in closed containers
- 📝 Data science content: [@jplimags.ds](https://www.instagram.com/jplimags.ds/)
- 💬 Happy to talk about: ML in healthcare/insurance, MLOps, applied AI research
